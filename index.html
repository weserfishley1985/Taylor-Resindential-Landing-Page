Clean architectural lines, concealed panel fasteners, and an interlocking design make Easy-Lock™ a practical choice for residential roofs and well-planned DIY projects.

Why homeowners choose Easy-Lock™

  • Designed for curb appeal

    Softer standing-seam lines and flat, accent-rib or striated panel options complement a wide range of home styles.

  • Protected panel fasteners

    Attachment points sit beneath the seams, keeping fasteners concealed and the roof’s appearance clean.

  • A purposeful seam design

    The patented no-siphon dry-lock seam is designed as part of a weather-tight roofing system.

  • Room for natural movement

    The pre-slotted fastening flange accommodates expansion and contraction when panels are installed as specified.

The benefits of metal roofing

Why choose a metal roof?

A roof that complements your home today, with practical advantages for the years ahead.

  • Long service life. A properly installed and maintained metal roof can protect your home for decades.
  • Lower upkeep. Durable finishes help reduce routine maintenance, with periodic inspections and cleaning.
  • Energy-conscious options. Reflective finishes can reduce solar heat gain and cooling demand in suitable climates.
  • Weather-ready performance. Choose tested panel systems and roof assemblies suited to your local conditions.
  • Distinctive curb appeal. Clean rooflines and a choice of profiles, colors, and finishes complement your home's style.
  • Recyclable material. Metal can return to the recycling stream at the end of its service life.
